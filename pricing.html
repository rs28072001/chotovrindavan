PRICING FAQ

Common Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel from your account page and your access continues until the end of the billing period.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Patron plans are fully tax-deductible. Devotee plans include a partial receipt (service portion excluded). We issue receipts quarterly for all eligible members.

Can I upgrade later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from Seeker to Devotee or Patron at any time and we’ll pro-rate the difference on your next bill.

Do you offer family plans?

Devotee and Patron plans include benefits for a family of 4 at no extra cost. Please contact us for larger families or community groups.

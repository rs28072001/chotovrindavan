About this Course

This foundational course introduces you to classical Hatha Yoga postures, breathing techniques (pranayama), and meditation. Perfect for beginners and anyone looking to build a strong, mindful practice rooted in the Indian spiritual tradition.

You’ll learn alignment principles, breath-led movement, and end each class with a guided meditation. By the end of the six weeks you’ll have a complete 30-minute home practice you can maintain for life.

What You’ll Learn

  • Proper alignment in 30+ foundational asanas
  • Six pranayama techniques for energy and calm
  • How to design a daily home practice
  • The eight limbs of yoga (Ashtanga Yoga)
  • Mantra japa and classical meditation
  • Safe modifications for every body type

Course Curriculum

  • Introduction to Hatha Yoga & temple tradition
  • Basic standing postures (Tadasana, Uttanasana)
  • Three-part breath & Ujjayi pranayama
  • Warrior series (Virabhadrasana I, II, III)
  • Tree pose (Vrikshasana) variations
  • Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing)
  • Paschimottanasana & Janu Sirsasana
  • Bound angle pose (Baddha Konasana)
  • Introduction to meditation
  • Bhujangasana, Ushtrasana, Setu Bandhasana
  • Chest opening pranayama
  • Heart chakra meditation
  • Supported Sarvangasana (shoulder stand)
  • Core strengthening postures
  • Kapalabhati (skull shining breath)
  • Full 30-minute home flow
  • Mantra japa with tulsi mala
  • Closing ceremony & certificates

Our Story

A Place of Devotion, Learning and Service

Our temple is more than a place of worship — it is a vibrant community that celebrates faith, culture and compassion. Inspired by the timeless teachings of Lord Krishna, we strive to create a space where everyone can connect, learn and grow together.

Our Purpose

Rooted in Dharma, Serving the World

Guided by timeless values, we work towards a more compassionate, connected and harmonious society.

Our Mission

To promote spiritual growth, preserve our rich cultural heritage and serve the community through education, seva and compassionate action.

Our Vision

A world inspired by the teachings of Lord Krishna — where everyone lives with peace, purpose and kindness.

Our Values

Devotion · Integrity · Inclusivity · Service · Learning · Community

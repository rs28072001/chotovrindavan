Your donation becomes a meal

Your generous contribution helps us organise Vishal Bhandara and serve fresh, nutritious and sattvic meals to devotees, pilgrims and visitors. The seva includes raw ingredients, cooking, serving, drinking water, utensils and all arrangements required for a smooth bhandara.

Every donation, big or small, becomes a part of this sacred service and helps feed many with love and devotion.

What your donation will provide

  • ₹51

    Helps provide prasad / meal ingredients for devotees.

  • ₹101

    Supports meals for several people.

  • ₹501

    Helps with a larger batch of bhandara preparations.

  • ₹1,100

    Supports a substantial bhandara contribution.

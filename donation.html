Every child deserves a classroom

Choto Vrindavan Dham Trust has been serving remote villages for more than a decade. Our current campaign funds the construction of a new learning centre in the foothills of Rishikesh — complete with desks, books, clean drinking water, and a qualified teacher for the first full academic year.

Your donation is fully tax-deductible and directly supports on-ground operations. Every rupee collected is accounted for in our public quarterly reports, and donors above $100 receive a personal update letter from the classroom once construction begins.

What your donation will fund
  • $15 — Supplies a child with notebooks and pencils for a full term
  • $45 — Covers a weekly hot meal programme for 10 students
  • $100 — Funds one desk and chair for the new classroom
  • $500 — Sponsors a child's full academic year including uniform and tuition
